What each region is

The 17 streams, top-to-bottom exactly as in the drawing — ordered by their median position in the optimized order, which happens to be the path a signal takes from the senses to the muscles. Click any lane name in the drawing to jump straight to its entry. A caveat for the analogies: they are functional ("plays a similar role"), not evolutionary — insect and human brains diverged ~600 million years ago.

How this page is computed

One graph, one order: the FlyWire MFAS-challenge fly connectome (136,648 neurons, 5,657,719 edges, total weight 41,912,141 synapses) and the pinned H64 champion order π, re-scored by the frozen oracle to 84.2582 % feedforward before anything is drawn. An edge is feedforward iff its source precedes its target in π — the class is fixed at node level and no layout choice here can relabel an edge.

Streams grid

Lanes are biologically named streams from the public FlyWire annotations: super_class refined by cell_class. Optic lanes are named by the input neuropil encoded in cell_class (LA>ME takes input in the lamina). Lanes are ordered top-to-bottom by their members' median π rank — the ordering is data-driven, and it recovers the textbook sensory→motor pathway (photoreceptors 7.6k → lamina 24.9k → medulla 46.2k → lobula 76.5k → visual projection 110.6k → descending 130.8k → motor 136.5k; the neck motor neuron is the last node of the whole order). A permutation test on the annotation's flow field (afferent < intrinsic < efferent vs π rank): Spearman ρ = 0.263, p ≤ 5·10⁻⁴ (2,000 label permutations, 0 exceedances, n = 136,635).

Columns are equal-count contiguous slices of π, so bins inherit π's direction: a feedforward edge always lands rightward-or-vertical, feedback leftward-or-vertical. Cells are (lane, bin) groups: area ~ neurons (each side scales with √n; floored for visibility — tiny cells are drawn larger than scale), border thickness ~ intra-cell weight, hatched grey = >50 % of members have no outputs in this graph (truncated fragments in sensory lanes; genuine terminals for motor/endocrine — 2,447 pure sinks, 6,651 pure sources in the audit).

Bundles sum edge weight between cells and are split into their FF and FB parts. Feedforward carries the source lane's colour; feedback is always crimson, drawn on top, same width/alpha law (~√weight). Same-lane bundles bow over (FF) / under (FB) their lane with arc height ~ bin distance. A direction is inked iff its own weight passes the threshold slider; the live readout under the slider states exactly what share of inter-cell FF and FB weight is currently visible — at the default 0.02 % threshold and 16 bins that is 85 % of FF but only 53 % of FB, because feedback is diffuse. The lane matrix view is the honesty backstop: it contains every gram of inter-lane weight, thresholded or not.

Hidden inside cells (not drawable as bundles): 11.9 % FF + 2.5 % FB of total weight at 16 bins (FF+FB totals: 18.9 % at 8 bins, 14.4 % at 16, 10.9 % at 32). Known taxonomy simplifications: "lobula (plate)" merges LO and LOP (feature vs motion channels); "photoreceptors" includes the four sensory/optic_lobes cells; "mushroom body" pools Kenyon cells with DAN/MBON/MBIN — hover a cell to see its actual cell-type composition. Lane order under mean instead of median moves 6 of 17 lanes (drivers: the 205-node "optic (other)" and the 22k "central (other)" grab-bag, both heavily skewed); median follows the mass, not the tail.

Neuron layer

Every neuron is one dot: x = its π rank (aligned with the bins, which are linear in rank), y = its lane band plus the within-lane rank of a spectral affinity coordinate — the leading non-trivial eigenvector of the τ-regularized random-walk operator (D+τI)⁻¹(W+τ/n·J) on the undirected log1p-weight graph (τ = mean weighted degree ≈ 138.9, seed 42, 300 deflated power iterations; unregularized vectors localize on weakly attached sensory fragments). Vertical position inside a band is ordinal, not metric. Validation: with no supervision, the coordinate visibly splits left from right hemisphere inside the optic lanes (judged by eye in the hemisphere view, not by a per-lane statistic) — switch the layer to "hemisphere" to see it. The dense terminal stripe at the far right of several lanes is the zero-out-weight sink pile made per-neuron-visible.

The type-highlight search matches the annotation's cell_type; the 1,233 neurons without one appear under their cell class instead, labelled "… (class)" — picking such an entry highlights only the untyped members of that class, not the whole class.

Lane matrix

The 17×17 lane-to-lane weight matrix (source = row). "% of weight" shows each pair's share of total synapse weight (diagonal = intra-lane); "FF share" colours each pair by what fraction of its weight is feedforward — crimson-dominated cells below the diagonal are the retro-projections (e.g. visual projection → medulla is only 38 % FF). Unlike the bundle drawing, nothing is thresholded away.
