What each lane is

The ten lanes, top-to-bottom exactly as in the drawing — here the order is anatomical (pia to white matter, excitatory and inhibitory sub-lanes per depth band), not fitted to the optimizer. That is deliberate: any diagonal structure you see in Streams is a measured relation between the optimized order and real cortical depth. The lane bands are our density-calibrated depth bands, not tracings of layer borders — see Methods.

How this page is computed

One graph, one order: the MICrONS minnie65 connectome (67,534 neurons, 10,436,569 directed connections, total weight 15,400,557 synapses; v117 public release, weight = synapse count per neuron pair) and the pinned H42 champion order π, re-scored by the frozen oracle to 83.2409 % feedforward before anything is drawn. An edge is feedforward iff its source precedes its target in π — the class is fixed at node level and no layout choice here can relabel an edge. This page is the sibling of "Fly Connectome Streams" (136,648 neurons, 84.26 % feedforward): same optimizer, same drawing rules, a very different brain.

The one big caveat first: axons are truncated

Only 249 of 67,534 neurons have a proofread axon in this release; every other neuron's outgoing wiring is an automatically segmented fragment. The median proofread neuron carries 341 outgoing synapses in this graph against 70 for the population — outgoing weight is undersampled roughly everywhere, and 1,205 neurons have no outputs at all (the optimizer necessarily dumps them at the end of the order, median rank at the 99th percentile; they are the hatched cells and the "reconstruction" dots). Long-range axons also leave the 1.4 × 0.9 mm volume entirely, so "output" here always means local output. Directional statements on this page inherit this caveat; the within-class direction splits are the robust part, absolute class weight shares the fragile one.

Anatomy joins

Soma positions come from the public nucleus table (100 % of graph nodes matched); excitatory/inhibitory labels from the anatomy-based allen_soma_ei_class_model_v1 table (E 55,855 · I 7,219 · unclassified 4,460, of which 663 are merged segments with conflicting labels; 5,953 roots carry more than one soma). Depth below pia subtracts a pia proxy — the 1st percentile of excitatory soma height on a 100-µm (x,z) grid, hole-filled and smoothed (the pia is tilted and gently curved in raw coordinates — ~5° mean slope, 397 µm of measured relief — and the grid removes both). A residual check: after correction, depth still correlates weakly with z (Spearman −0.16), so treat ±30 µm structure near band borders with care.

Layer bands are calibrated, not traced. No public flat file carries minnie65's layer borders, so we anchor them on landmarks of the smoothed excitatory-soma density profile — the L4 peak (288 µm), the L3 and L5a troughs, the L6 peak (642 µm) — via half-height flank crossings: L1/L2-3 at 61, L2-3/L4 at 223, L4/L5 at 393, L5/L6 at 563, L6/WM at 778 µm. The full profile ships in this page's data. Bands are display annotation; they never touch the optimizer or the scorer.

Streams grid

Lanes are (depth band × E/I): four excitatory lanes (blue, darker = deeper), four inhibitory lanes (green; L1's few hundred excitatory cells are pooled into L2/3 · exc, its interneurons into the upper inhibitory lane), plus white matter and unclassified (grey). Lane order is fixed anatomically — pia at the top — so the drawing cannot manufacture a hierarchy. Columns are equal-count contiguous slices of π; a feedforward edge always lands rightward-or-vertical, feedback leftward-or-vertical. Cells: area ~ neurons (sides ~√n, floored), border ~ intra-cell weight, hatched = >50 % of members have zero outputs here. Bundles sum inter-cell weight, split into FF (source-lane colour) and FB (crimson, on top, same width/alpha law ~√weight). A direction is inked iff it passes the threshold slider; the live readout states exactly what share of FF and FB weight is visible. The Matrix view is the honesty backstop — nothing is thresholded away there.

In the neuron layer, a dot's y inside its lane is its soma depth within the lane's own depth range (metric µm, unlike the fly page's ordinal spectral coordinate); its x is the π rank, aligned with the bins.

What the order finds (all numbers recomputable)

  • The order descends the cortex. Excitatory rank vs soma depth: Spearman ρ = 0.356 (n = 55,855; 2,000 label permutations, 0 exceedances, p ≤ 5·10⁻⁴). Median ranks: L2/3 18.5k → L4 26.9k → L5 36.4k → L6 46.9k. This is monotone with depth — not the canonical thalamus→L4→L2/3→L5 story, and it cannot be: the thalamus is outside the graph, so L4's role as the entry point is invisible to the optimizer.
  • Interneurons sort early. Every inhibitory lane sits 7–13k ranks before its excitatory partner (upper I median 11.5k vs L2/3 E 18.5k; per-band median gaps 6.7–12.9k). The mechanism is arithmetic, and the Balance view shows it: I→E carries 33.9 % of ALL weight and the order makes 97.3 % of it forward, while E→I carries 9.4 % at 48.4 % forward. A loop must pay its backward cost somewhere; the optimizer puts it on the lighter side — exactly how the fly page's visual-centrifugal cells were "resolved", here scaled to an entire cell class.
  • The fly's chemistry finding reverses. In the fly, backward weight was enriched for inhibitory/modulatory transmitters. Here, with anatomical labels, inhibitory output is 5.2 % backward and excitatory 26.6 % — feedback is carried by excitation. (Weight-share caveat: inhibitory axons are local and well-sampled, excitatory axons truncated, so class shares are biased toward inhibition.)
  • Feedback is long-range in the order here. Within ±1.7 % of the order the fly holds 19 % of its weight; this graph holds 5.5 % (a whole brain against a 1.4-mm patch — completeness differs). No feedback hub: the top receiving neuron takes 0.13 % of backward weight (top 1 % of neurons ≈ 20 %, similar to the fly's concentration).
  • No horizontal sorting. Rank vs x (the axis crossing V1 into higher visual areas): ρ = +0.05 among E cells — the optimizer does not rediscover area boundaries; the hierarchy it finds is radial (depth), not tangential. (Rank vs z: −0.24, partly the −0.16 depth residual.)
