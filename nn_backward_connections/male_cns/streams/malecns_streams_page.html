How this page is computed

One graph, one order. The graph is the Janelia FlyEM Male CNS v1.0 connectome (brain and ventral nerve cord of one male fly; Berg et al. 2025 preprint, CC BY): traced-only neuron pairs, weight = synapse count, edges with weight >= 2 kept (the same floor as our FlyWire graph), self-loops dropped. That gives neurons, edges and synapses in total. The order π is the output of our H64 pipeline (the FlyWire champion pipeline with unchanged constants), run once with seed 42 on Apple MPS. The frozen oracle re-scores it to synapses,  % feedforward, before anything is drawn. An edge is feedforward if its source comes before its target in π. This class is fixed per edge, so no layout choice here can relabel an edge.

One seed on a non-deterministic device: treat positions as one good order, not as the best order. The FlyWire page uses the CUDA champion (84.26 %).

Streams grid

Lanes use the FlyWire lane vocabulary, mapped from the Male CNS annotation by male_cns/pipeline/ann_prep.py. Optic-lobe neurons have no neuropil class in the Male CNS, so each takes the FlyWire cell_class of its matched flywireType (majority over FlyWire neurons of that type), and then the FlyWire optic rule applies (lane named by the input neuropil). Optic neurons with no match go to "optic (other)". Central-brain neurons are split by class as in FlyWire. Four lanes are new: VNC sensory, VNC intrinsic and motor (VNC), because the Male CNS also contains the nerve cord, and unannotated (no superclass). FlyWire's "motor" is called motor (brain) here. Lanes are ordered top to bottom by their members' median π rank. A permutation test on the flow field (afferent < intrinsic < efferent vs π rank, flow from the Male CNS superclass): Spearman ρ = , p ≤ ( label permutations, exceedances, n = ). Descending neurons count as intrinsic here (in the Male CNS they connect brain and cord).

Columns are equal-count contiguous slices of π, so a feedforward edge always lands rightward or vertical and feedback leftward or vertical. Cells are (lane, bin) groups: area ~ neurons (each side scales with √n, floored for visibility), border thickness ~ intra-cell weight, hatched grey = more than 50 % of members have no outputs in this graph ( pure sinks, pure sources; of the sinks are medulla neurons, mostly T1 and Lawf2).

Bundles sum edge weight between cells, split into FF and FB parts. Feedforward carries the source lane's colour; feedback is always crimson, drawn on top, same width and alpha law (~√weight). Same-lane bundles bow over (FF) or under (FB) their lane. A direction is inked if its own weight passes the threshold slider; the readout under the slider states what share of inter-cell FF and FB weight is visible. At the default 0.02 % threshold and 16 bins that is  % of FF but only  % of FB, because feedback is diffuse. The lane matrix view contains every gram of inter-lane weight, thresholded or not.

Hidden inside cells (not drawable as bundles) at 16 bins:  % FF +  % FB of total weight (FF+FB:  % at 8 bins,  % at 16,  % at 32). Lane order under mean instead of median moves of lanes; median follows the mass, not the tail.

Neuron layer

Every neuron is one dot: x = its π rank, y = its lane band plus the within-lane rank of a spectral affinity coordinate (the leading non-trivial eigenvector of the τ-regularized random-walk operator on the undirected log1p-weight graph, τ = mean weighted degree ≈ , seed 42, at most 300 power iterations; same code as FlyWire). Vertical position inside a band is ordinal, not metric. Hemisphere comes from the Male CNS somaSide.

The type search matches the Male CNS type; the neurons without one appear under their class (or superclass), labelled "… (class)".

Lane matrix

The × lane-to-lane weight matrix (source = row). "% of weight" shows each pair's share of total synapse weight (diagonal = intra-lane); "FF share" colours each pair by the fraction of its weight that is feedforward. Nothing is thresholded away.

Feedback is disproportionately inhibitory

Composition of edge weight by the source neuron's predicted transmitter:

top_nt is the Male CNS consensus transmitter prediction, not ground truth; "unclear" counts as unlabeled, and Kenyon cells are set to acetylcholine (their dopamine prediction is a known classifier error, as in FlyWire). GABA and glutamate are mostly inhibitory in the fly. Histamine is not predicted in FlyWire.

Compared with FlyWire (female brain)

Both orders come from the same H64 pipeline (female: CUDA champion; male: one seed on MPS). The male graph includes the nerve cord and has about 2.3 times more synapses per neuron, so differences mix sex, dataset and size. Full analysis: male_cns/README.md.
