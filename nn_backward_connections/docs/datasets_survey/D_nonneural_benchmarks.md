# D. FAS / LOP benchmarks and non-neural directed networks (survey, 2026-11-17)

Scope: (1) FAS / LOP benchmark instances with published numbers, (2) non-neural real directed
weighted networks where unavoidable feedback / hierarchy is studied, (3) ANNs as graphs,
(4) graph repositories.

Conventions. "FF%" = forward weight / total weight. Percentages marked "(computed)" are my own
arithmetic from published absolute numbers; everything else is quoted from the cited source.
UNVERIFIED = not checked against the primary source in this session. Fit score 1-5 = usefulness
for our thesis (weighted, large sparse, published comparator, or meaningful science question).

## 1. FAS / LOP benchmarks

### 1.1 ISCAS / ISPD98 circuit graphs (best published comparator with exact optima) - Fit 4

- Xiong, Zhou, Xiao, Khoussainov 2024, "Finding small feedback arc sets on large graphs", Computers & OR 169:106724
  (RASstar). Says reductions alone reach optimum on ~80% of ISCAS instances and two large social networks; D&C improves
  average FAS by 10% (social) and 31% (circuits) over baselines. Which social networks: UNVERIFIED (paywalled).

### 1.2 Baharev et al. exact MFAS test set - Fit 2
- Baharev, Schichl, Neumaier, Achterberg, "An Exact Method for the Minimum FAS Problem", ACM JEA 26(1) 1.4, 2021,
  doi:10.1145/3446429. 4,468 test graphs with minimum FAS: 3,737 sparse Erdős–Rényi G(n, c/n), generalized de Bruijn,
  Imase-Itoh (Kautz) graphs, 707 random tournaments, 11 chemical-engineering graphs (n ≤ 109). Unweighted.
  Download: github via https://sdopt-tearing.readthedocs.io (Baharev "Exact and Heuristic Methods for Tearing").
- Geladaris-Lionakis-Tollis (JGAA 27(8) 2023) reuse the hardest ones and report heuristic vs exact (Tables 1-4).
- Use: small exact unit tests for our local search only. Not a scale benchmark.

### 1.3 Web-scale unweighted FAS (Simpson et al. 2016; PageRankFAS 2023) - Fit 3
- Simpson, Srinivasan, Thomo, "Efficient computation of feedback arc set at web-scale", PVLDB 10(3):133-144, 2016.
  Code: github.com/stamps/FAS (WebGraph). Datasets from LAW (https://law.di.unimi.it/datasets.php):
  wordassociation-2011 (10,617 / 72,172), enron (69,244 / 276,143), uk-2007-05@100000 (100,000 / 3,050,615),
  cnr-2000 (325,557 / 3,216,152), uk-2002 (18.5M / 298M), arabic-2005 (22.7M / 640M), uk-2005 (39.5M / 936M),
  webbase-2001, twitter-2010, clueweb12 (42B arcs).
  Published FAS fraction (of arcs): GreedyFAS 18.89% wordassoc, 12.54% enron; SortFAS 20.17% / 14.16%;
  GreedyFAS ~10% on uk-2007@100k (BergerShor 13%); clueweb12 GreedyFAS 3.6% vs BergerShor 16%.
- Geladaris, Lionakis, Tollis, "Effective Computation of a FAS Using PageRank", JGAA 27(8):737-757 (2023; GD 2022):
  PageRankFAS 14.85% wordassoc, 11.05% enron, 9.17% uk-2007@100k (GreedyFAS 10.23%).
- Use: real, unweighted, published heuristic numbers we can beat to show generality at 10^5-10^6 nodes.
  Weak comparators (no strong local search was published there). License: LAW datasets free for research.

### 1.4 LOLIB (linear ordering problem) - Fit 2
- Optsicom: https://grafo.etsii.urjc.es/optsicom/lolib.html, best-known values `lolib_bestvalues.zip`.
  Sets: IO 50 (n 44-79, real input-output tables), SGB 25 (n 75), RandA1 100 (100-500), RandA2 75, RandB 90 (40-50),
  MB 30 (100-250), xLOLIB 78 (150-250), Special 37 (incl. econ, atp tennis, Paley), xLOLIB2 200 (300-1000).
  Martí's PDF of best values: https://www.uv.es/rmarti/paper/results/Best%20LOP%20Results.pdf.
  New best-knowns for 77/78 xLOLIB: arXiv:2106.02696 (diversity-aware memetic). Optsicom file may be stale.
- New 2026 benchmark: Fagiolo, Baioletti, Santucci, "Linear Ordering Problem: Time for a Change", arXiv:2605.31051
  (June 2026). 176 EXIOBASE-derived instances: pxp 49 (n 89-176), rxr 28 (n 49), isic 49 (n 10), os300 49 (n 300).
  Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.20554376. Compared CD-RVNS and MA-EDM. No license stated.
- Use: dense, small. LOP = weighted MFAS on complete digraph. Good for "do we match state-of-the-art on a mature
  benchmark" but our method is built for sparse large graphs; expect to lose to memetic LOP solvers. IO set is also
  a science point (economies are ~triangular).

### 1.5 Pairwise-comparison / ranking instances (weighted, small) - Fit 3
- He et al., GNNRank, ICML 2022 (arXiv:2202.00211), code github.com/SherylHYX/GNNRank. Datasets: Animal (21/193),
  Faculty Business (113/1787), CS (206/1407), History (145/1204), Football / finer (20 nodes), Basketball 1985-2014
  (282-351 nodes, 2.9-7.7k edges), HeadToHead (602/5002), Finance (1315 / 1.73M dense).
- Vahidi & Koutis, arXiv:2412.16181 (J. Supercomputing 2026): MWFAS heuristic on 107 of these; reports naive/simple/
  ratio upset losses vs GNNRank (e.g. Basketball_1985 naive upset 0.11 vs GNNRank 0.18; HeadToHead 0.18 vs 0.24).
  Weighted edge lists: github.com/SoroushVahidi/Ranking_with_MWFAS.
- "Learning-Free Ranking via FAS Pruning and Add-Back" (ResearchGate, 2026) and Cavallaro-Cutello-Pavone
  weighted MS-FAS (SEKE 2025, doi:10.18293/SEKE2025-049; J Comb Optim 48:30 2024) are related. Details UNVERIFIED.
- Use: weighted, published baseline (upset loss = backward fraction). Small, quick to add as a sanity suite.

### 1.6 Kemeny rank aggregation (PrefLib) - Fit 1
- Kemeny = weighted FAS on a tournament (dense). Exact work: Betzler et al. data reduction (PrefLib instances),
  arXiv:2506.15097, arXiv:2511.19345. No standard table of best values found. Dense complete graphs; off-target.

### 1.7 PACE 2022 Directed Feedback Vertex Set - Fit 1
- https://pacechallenge.org/2022/results/ ; report Großmann et al., IPEC 2022 LIPIcs 249:26. 200 instances,
  exact track (Schidler & Kiesel, 185/200), heuristic track (DiVerSeS by S. Swat; DreyFVS, hust_huawei next).
  Vertex version, unweighted. Only useful as related-literature context (reduction rules, lazy cycle constraints).

### 1.8 Other FAS literature checked
- Brandenburg & Hanauer, "Sorting heuristics for the FAS problem", Passau MIP-1104 (2011): random graphs only
  (1000 graphs per config), plus counterexample graphs. No public real benchmark. Fit 1.
- Eades-Lin-Smyth 1993: algorithm only, no instance set.
- Kudelić: Monte-Carlo (Appl. Soft Comput. 41, 2016), ant-inspired (ESWA 122, 2019), book 2022. Test graphs from
  information systems, not public as a standard set. Fit 1.
- Hecht 2018 "Exact Localisations of Feedback Sets" (Theory Comput Syst 62): theory, exact DP for weighted case.
- Zhao & Zhou, "Feedback arcs and node hierarchy in directed networks", Chin. Phys. B 26:078901 (arXiv:1612.05347):
  BPD and SA on random graphs and real networks; Florida food web gives far fewer feedback arcs than direction-randomized
  null (601 expected). Directly our "real vs null" question. Fit 3 (numbers per network UNVERIFIED).

## 2. Non-neural real directed weighted networks (science comparison points)

Measures used in literature: trophic incoherence q (Johnson 2014) or F0 (MacKay-Johnson-Sansom 2020), agony
(Gupte 2011, Tatti), minimum violation ranking (MVR) = unweighted FAS on multigraph, triangle transitivity (animal
dominance). Only MVR and FAS heuristics are the same objective as ours. Note: agony ≥ FAS-type cost (each backward
or same-rank edge pays ≥ 1), so it is a different, coarser hierarchy measure.

### 2.1 Faculty hiring networks - Fit 5 (best "same objective, published, with null model")
- Clauset, Arbesman, Larremore, Sci. Adv. 1:e1400005 (2015). Business 112 inst., History 144, CS 205; mean degree
  70/29/21. MVR: "only 9 to 14% of faculty are placed at institutions more prestigious than their doctorate
  (ρ = 0.86 to 0.91)" (ρ = fraction of unviolated edges). Fig. S2 compares ρ against a degree-preserving null model.
  This is exactly "how much feedback is unavoidable vs random graph with same degrees".
- Wapman et al., Nature 2022 (US faculty hiring 2011-2020). On Netzschleuder as `faculty_hiring_us`
  (3,284 nodes, 61,936 edges, directed, weighted). Upward-hire fractions per field: UNVERIFIED.
- Also `faculty_hiring` (206/4,988) on Netzschleuder; GNNRank weighted versions (1.5).
- Akbari, Escobedo, Sefair, arXiv:2608.07755 (Aug 2026): bidirectionality-preserving FAS, IEOR hiring data.

### 2.2 Food webs (weighted carbon-flow webs) - Fit 4
- Johnson, Domínguez-García, Donetti, Muñoz, PNAS 111:17923 (2014), doi:10.1073/pnas.1409077111: 46 food webs, q
  vs stability. Johnson & Jones, PNAS 114:5618 (2017), arXiv:1505.07332: 42 food webs q/q̃ = 0.44 ± 0.17,
  31/42 acyclic; gene regulatory 8 nets q/q̃ = 0.99 ± 0.05 (1/8 acyclic); metabolic 7 nets 1.81 ± 0.11 (0/7 acyclic).
  Misc table: C. elegans neural (N 297) q/q̃ = 0.42; P2P Gnutella08 (6301) 0.98; trade manufactured (24) 1.14,
  minerals 1.02; words (50) 1.01. Data: https://www.samuel-johnson.org/data (Rodgers et al. 2023 cite this).
  Mostly unweighted (binary adjacency).
- MacKay, Johnson, Sansom, R. Soc. Open Sci. 7:201138 (2020): F0 for any directed network; Ythan estuary F0 = 0.08.
- Rodgers, Tiňo, Johnson, PNAS 120:e2215752120 (2023), arXiv:2208.08259: counts edges pointing backward in trophic
  level (an upper bound on FAS) vs F0 across food webs, genetic, metabolic, neural, trade nets.
- Weighted webs: Ulanowicz carbon-flow webs (Florida Bay wet/dry 128 nodes, 2,106/2,137 edges; Everglades graminoids
  69/915; Mangrove, Cypress, St Marks 54/356, Chesapeake). Netzschleuder `foodweb_ulanowicz` (21 networks, 2026)
  and `foodweb_baywet`; R igraphdata `foodwebs`; Pajek data. Also `messal_shale` (700/6,444), `mangal` collection.
- Use: weighted, small, strong prior expectation of near-acyclicity. Easy to run our pipeline + degree/weight nulls.
  Contrast: food webs are much more coherent than random, brains likely the opposite or in-between.

### 2.3 Gene regulatory and metabolic networks - Fit 3
- GRN: E. coli RegulonDB, yeast Harbison, Alon lab collection (wws.weizmann.ac.il/mcb/UriAlon/download/collection-complex-networks).
  Nearly acyclic, unweighted, q/q̃ ≈ 1 (Johnson & Jones). Small FAS, likely trivial for our method.
- Metabolic (Jeong et al. 2000, 43 organisms; Notre Dame data): incoherent, strongly cyclic. Unweighted, n ~400-1500.
- Use: two poles of the "feedback" spectrum for a comparison figure. Weights absent.

### 2.4 Input-output economic tables and world trade - Fit 4
- Classic: triangulation of IO tables = LOP (Chenery & Watanabe 1958). LOLIB IO set (European IO tables, n 44-79).
- Large weighted MRIO: WIOD 2016 (43 countries + RoW x 56 sectors ≈ 2,464 nodes), EXIOBASE 3 (49 regions x 200
  products ≈ 9,800 nodes, dense), OECD ICIO. Sizes from memory, UNVERIFIED. Licenses: WIOD CC BY 4.0, EXIOBASE
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (UNVERIFIED).
- EXIOBASE LOP instances: arXiv:2605.31051, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20554376 (see 1.4).
- Hierarchy literature: upstreamness (Antràs et al.), arXiv:2303.06603, 2009.06350 (WIOD), MacKay-Johnson-Sansom
  trophic incoherence of national IO networks (Rebuilding Macroeconomics project; values UNVERIFIED).
- Trade: Netzschleuder `fao_trade` (214 / 318,346, multilayer), `metal_trade` (80/1,000); BACI-CEPII.
- Use: weighted, dense-ish; economically meaningful "how triangular is the economy". Dense matrices need pruning
  of tiny flows before our sparse code is efficient.

### 2.5 Animal dominance and sports - Fit 3
- DomArchive: Strauss et al., Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B 377:20200436 (2022). ~436 win/loss matrices, 367 groups,
  mean group size 13.8, R package. Weighted (counts). Tiny graphs; FAS is cheap/exact. Netzschleuder `dom`.
- Shizuka & McDonald 2012 (triangle transitivity, 0 of 101 networks near random), McDonald & Shizuka 2013,
  Shizuka & McDonald 2015 J R Soc Interface.
- Jerdee & Newman, Sci. Adv. 10:eadn2654 (2024): depth/luck across sports and human/animal hierarchies;
  data Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.kh18932fc, code github.com/maxjerdee/pairwise-ranking.
- SpringRank (De Bacco, Larremore, Moore, Sci. Adv. 2018, arXiv:1709.09002) compares to MVR on these datasets.
- Use: good "social hierarchy" contrast group. Exact MFAS is possible on them (n ≤ ~100), so no algorithmic stress.

### 2.6 Social / web / communication (agony literature) - Fit 3
- Tatti, "Tiers for peers", DMKD (arXiv:1903.02999), code users.ics.aalto.fi/ntatti/agony.zip. Datasets (|V|,|E|):
  Amazon 403,394/3,387,388; Gnutella 62,586/147,892; EmailEU 265,214/418,956; Epinions 75,879/508,837;
  Slashdot 82,168/870,161; WebGoogle 875,713/5,105,039; WikiVote 7,115/103,689; Nfl 32/205 (weighted);
  Reef 258/4,232; HiggsReply/Retweet/Mention (weighted). Optimal agony has 10-20 levels typically.
  Agony values are in the paper's figures/tables, not extracted here. Most are on SNAP.
- Gupte et al., WWW 2011 "Finding hierarchy in directed online social networks".
- Use: SNAP graphs at 10^5-10^6 scale, mostly unweighted; Higgs retweet/mention weighted. Same scale as FlyWire.

### 2.7 Software dependencies - Fit 2
- FAS used in practice for cycle breaking (Sonargraph; Debian CircularBuildDependencies). Maven Central graph
  (arXiv:1901.05392, ~1.4M nodes per arXiv:2508.13819) is mostly a DAG with local SCCs. No published FAS numbers
  found. Netzschleuder `cpan` (2,740/5,426), `metamath_setmm` (51,242/1.5M; theorem dependencies, acyclic by construction).
- Use: weak; acyclic by design so the "unavoidable feedback" question is near-trivial.

### 2.8 Citation, law, Wikipedia, transport, mobility - Fit 1-2
- Citations (SNAP cit-HepPh, courtlistener_citations 8.1M/78M weighted, jlaw_citegraph 951k/1.5M): almost a DAG by time.
- Wikipedia hyperlinks (dbpedia_link 18M/172M): huge, unweighted-ish; cycle-rich. Too large for a fair study.
- Transport/mobility (insee_commuting 38,130/1.01M, at_migrations, citibike, divvy, US air traffic): flows nearly
  symmetric, so FF ≈ 50%+small; little hierarchy. Low scientific value for the brain comparison.

## 3. Artificial neural networks as graphs - Fit 2 (conceptual only)

- No paper found that runs an MFAS / trophic analysis on trained ANN weight graphs. Feed-forward nets are DAGs by
  construction, so only recurrent nets are interesting.
- "Hidden feedforward" in recurrent connectivity via Schur decomposition: Goldman, Neuron 61:621 (2009); Murphy &
  Miller, Neuron 61:635 (2009); commentary Ganguli & Latham. Here feedforward is between activity patterns (Schur
  vectors), not neurons; an MFAS ordering is the neuron-space analogue.
- Trained RNNs develop chain-like feedforward structure: Rajan, Harvey, Tank, Neuron 90:128 (2016); Hardy &
  Buonomano, Neural Comput. 2018; Orhan & Pitkow, ICLR 2020 (arXiv:1905.13715; seen even from orthogonal init).
  They average W by (i - j) after sorting by peak time, which is a crude FF ordering.
- Trophic analysis of directed sparse Hopfield nets: arXiv:2202.00350 (on binary wiring, not weights).
- Possible own experiment: train vanilla RNNs, take |W|, run our MFAS, compare FF% vs random-init and vs shuffled.
  This would be new; no published comparator.

## 5. Recommended picks (ranked)

1. FlyWire leaderboard top-5 submission CSVs: score them with our oracle; study where they differ from ours
   (target to beat is now 35,544,233 = 84.807%, not 84.61%).
2. ISCAS + ISPD98 (TIGHT-CUT* table): exact optima for most; ibm01/ibm02/ibm05 (13k-29k nodes) as sparse
   mid-scale tests. Run unweighted.
3. Faculty hiring (Clauset 2015 + Wapman 2022 on Netzschleuder): same objective (MVR), published % and a
   degree-preserving null. Best science analogue for "real vs random feedback".
4. Weighted food webs (Ulanowicz set) + Johnson & Jones q/q̃ tables: a "very hierarchical" pole with literature values.
5. LAW web graphs (enron, wordassociation, uk-2007@100k, cnr-2000): published GreedyFAS / PageRankFAS percentages,
   10^4-10^5 nodes, shows our method generalizes beyond brains.
6. GNNRank pairwise sets (weighted, with Vahidi upset losses) as a small weighted sanity suite.
7. Optional: LOLIB IO / EXIOBASE LOP for a dense-instance check; expect specialised LOP memetics to win.
