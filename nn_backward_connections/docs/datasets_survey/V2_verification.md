## Notes for the survey

- #8 changes meaning: in the optic lobe, feedback is the small class (12%) and lateral is large (43%). It is also a flow-layer classification on the male CNS, so it is not comparable to our MFAS feedback share.
- #7: Durbin's 4.8% is per synapse count on a class-level graph of the nerve ring, with a heuristic order. It is a prior max-forward sort, so the novelty risk stays; the "local" wording should be replaced with "mostly reciprocal".
